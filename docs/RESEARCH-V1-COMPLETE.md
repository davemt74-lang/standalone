# Annotated Research V1 — Feature Complete

Research V1 is feature-complete after Phase 30.

## Finished loop

**Capture → Investigate → Verify → Synthesize → Review → Publish → Monitor**

The finished system includes:

- Source and Annotation evidence capture
- Claims, exact evidence and Findings
- Claim / Entity graphs and Research briefs
- Agent-driven Research proposals with explicit confirmation
- Cognitive Feed and proactive Research intelligence
- Research automation
- Cross-Research intelligence
- Outcome Learning and Decision Memory
- Collaborative Research Review & Governance
- Change Impact & Staleness Intelligence
- Research Portfolio Intelligence
- Living Research publishing and distribution
- Research Network & immutable citation intelligence
- Research Provenance & Audit Ledger
- Research Trust, Review & Verification
- Research Reproducibility & Evidence Packs
- Unified Research Workflow

## Phase 30 hardening boundary

Phase 30 adds no new intelligence subsystem. It hardens the finished loop:

- only current, relevant Claim/Finding/Report reviews satisfy the workflow Review stage
- stale completed reviews no longer unlock Publish
- Agent-action reviews cannot substitute for human Research review
- Evidence Pack drift work is bounded in high-frequency Cognitive paths
- lifecycle navigation exposes current-step accessibility semantics
- Team revocation continues to remove access to historical private Research artifacts
- public Reports do not grant access to private project verification, packs or provenance
- Agent Research context remains project-scoped and bounded
- immutable Report snapshots, Evidence Packs and Audit Receipts retain hash verification and drift behavior
- Chrome and website release packaging are locked to v0.30.0

## Feature freeze

After Phase 30, the Research subsystem should receive bug fixes, performance improvements and evidence-backed usability changes, but no new major intelligence layers without a separate product decision.

The next product initiative should focus on the end-to-end Annotated journey across Browser → Annotation → Feed → Team → Research → Agent → Publish.
